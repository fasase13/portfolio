WASHQUEUE: A MOBILE-BASED QUEUING AND SCHEDULING APPLICATION FOR CAR WASH BUSINESSES

"Find a car wash nearby. Join the queue. Arrive when it's your turn."

WashQueue addresses the pervasive problem of unpredictable wait times and physical line congestion in the automotive cleaning industry. Built as a mobile-first Progressive Web App, it empowers customers to find nearby partner car wash stations, remotely secure their place in a virtual queue, choose specific wash services, and arrive precisely when a service bay opens.

Launch Live Web App (washqueue.online)
https://www.washqueue.online/

System Modules & Core Capabilities

Engineered for both vehicle owners and car wash business managers.

Virtual Queuing Engine

Remote queue registration allowing drivers to check current waiting times, see the number of vehicles ahead, and receive live progress alerts so they never waste time idling in physical queues.

Smart Scheduling & Bay Allocation

Appointment booking system that allows users to reserve future time slots, specify vehicle size (Sedan, SUV, Van, Motorcycle), and select tailored detailing packages.

Business Vendor Dashboard

Administrative interface enabling car wash operators to monitor active wash bays, update queue statuses, adjust staff service pacing, and analyze daily vehicle throughput.

Station Discovery & Geolocation

Proximity-based station locator highlighting operating hours, available service bays, pricing tables, and navigation directions for local car wash facilities.

PWA & Google Auth Bridge

Cross-platform Progressive Web App (PWA) with fast web installation, offline resilience, and friction-free user onboarding via Google Identity Services.

Turn & Arrival Notifications

Automated status notifications reminding customers when their designated wash bay is being prepped, ensuring seamless turnaround without bay idle time.
